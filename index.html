detour

Hides flight results that route through the US or its territories when you’re on Google Flights.

This tool is NOT meant as a sign of hostility against the United States.

Get the extension

Hi! I’m Koshin

Over the past year, my friends in Canada and beyond have expressed their concerns about travelling to the United States. Travel to the US has dropped drastically, driven by Canadians’ fears about border issues.

Detour is a simple extension. On Google Flights, it will hide flights with layovers in the US, giving you peace of mind about the flights you’re booking.

There are no analytics, account, or network requests involved. The only things stored are your on/off preference and the last result count on your own machine.
